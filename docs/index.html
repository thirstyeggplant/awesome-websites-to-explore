Featured: Aubergine - All your terminals on one canvas in your browser, to run several coding agents side by side and see which one is waiting for you. (Maintained by the owners of this list.)

Everything here works in an ordinary browser, needs no account to start, and costs nothing to try. Most of these sites are made by one person or a small team; if one charms you, tell them.

Take me somewhere random

Press a button, land on a site you have never seen. The spirit of StumbleUpon lives on in these.

  • Cloudhiker - Hops to a hand-picked site at random, with categories if you want to steer.
  • The Useless Web - One button that drops you on a single-joke website.
  • Viralwalk - Teleports you to odd, pretty or half-forgotten pages, filtered by mood.
  • Marginalia Explore - Wanders through small personal websites a search engine for the quiet web has found.
  • Kagi Small Web - Shows one recent post from an independent blog at a time.
  • indieblog.page - Sends you to a random post on a personal blog.
  • Wiby - A search engine for simple, old-style pages, with a "surprise me" button.
  • Neocities Browse - Thousands of homemade sites on a modern GeoCities, sorted by tag or by what changed lately.
  • MapCrunch - Drops you at a random Street View spot anywhere on Earth.

Interactive toys and explainers

  • Neal.fun - Playful projects about scale, money, space and the internet, such as scrolling down to the deepest point of the ocean.
  • The Pudding - Visual essays that answer odd questions with data you can poke at.
  • Bartosz Ciechanowski - Long articles on how watches, cameras and engines work, with models you turn and take apart.
  • Nicky Case - Small playable explanations of trust, anxiety, voting and other hard ideas.
  • Explorable Explanations - A directory of essays you learn from by moving things around.
  • Orb.Farm - A tiny aquarium you stock and keep alive.
  • Thisissand - Pour coloured sand into slow, layered landscapes.
  • Silk - Draw glowing symmetrical patterns with the pointer.
  • Zen Photon Garden - Place mirrors and walls and watch light bounce around them.
  • WebGL Fluid Simulation - Swirl bright fluid with your finger or mouse.
  • Falstad applets - Physics and electronics you can play with, from waves to circuits.
  • Koalas to the Max - Split circles by hovering until a picture appears.
  • The Scale of the Universe 2 - Zoom from the smallest known things to the size of the cosmos.
  • Experiments with Google - Hundreds of browser experiments with code, AI, music and maps.
  • VOLE.wtf - A pile of daft little games and toys from one inventive studio.

Travel from your chair

  • Radio Garden - Spin a globe and tune in to live local radio wherever you stop.
  • WindowSwap - Spend a few minutes at a stranger's window, in a city or a village you will never visit.
  • Drive & Listen - Ride through a city's streets on video while its local radio plays.
  • Virtual Vacation - Walk, drive or fly through cities on video, or tune into live cameras.
  • Yamanote.fun - The sounds of riding Tokyo's loop line, station by station.
  • Cities and Memory - A world map of field recordings, each paired with an artist's reworking.
  • Street View Fun - The strangest things map cars have caught on camera.
  • Atlas Obscura - Curious places around the world, mapped and described.
  • The True Size Of - Drag countries across the map to see how map projections distort them.

The planet and the sky, live

  • earth :: nullschool - An animated globe of wind, ocean currents and air quality as they are now.
  • Windy - Live weather maps of wind, rain, waves and storms.
  • LightningMaps - Lightning strikes appearing as they happen.
  • Flightradar24 - Planes in the air right now; click one to follow it.
  • Stellarium Web - The night sky over wherever you are, at any time.
  • NASA's Eyes - Fly along with spacecraft and planets in 3D.
  • meow.camera - Live cameras on cat feeders in China, with hungry visitors dropping by.

Music and sound

  • Chrome Music Lab - Simple instruments and experiments that make music theory visible.
  • Typatone - Every letter you type plays a note.
  • Patatap - Each key plays a sound and an animation.
  • Incredibox - Drag sounds onto a beatbox crew to build a track.
  • Multiplayer Piano - Play a piano in a room with strangers.
  • Online Sequencer - Compose tunes on a grid and share them.
  • Every Noise at Once - A map of thousands of music genres, each with a sample.
  • Radiooooo - Pick a country and a decade and hear what played there.
  • Music-Map - Type a band and see who sounds like them.
  • Gnoosic - Name three bands you like and it suggests a fourth.
  • myNoise - Hundreds of background soundscapes you tune with sliders.
  • A Soft Murmur - Mix rain, wind, fire and café sounds.
  • Rainy Mood - Rain and thunder, nothing more.
  • lofi.cafe - Relaxed beats over looping animations.
  • musicForProgramming(); - Long mixes made for concentrating.

Quick games

  • GeoGuessr - Guess where in the world a Street View picture was taken; the original, partly paid.
  • WorldGuessr - A free take on guessing places from Street View.
  • TimeGuessr - Guess where and in which year a photograph was taken.
  • Globle - Find the day's mystery country from how close each guess lands.
  • Contexto - Find the secret word from how close in meaning your guesses are.
  • Wiki Speedruns - Race from one Wikipedia article to another using only links.
  • The Endless Museum - Walk through Wikipedia as a 3D museum where each link is a door.
  • Little Alchemy 2 - Combine elements to discover hundreds of things.
  • Cookie Clicker - Click a cookie, then build an empire that clicks for you.
  • Kittens Game - A slow, text-based game of growing a village of cats.
  • Akinator - Think of someone; a genie guesses who from yes-or-no questions.
  • Find the Invisible Cow - Hunt for a hidden cow by sound alone.
  • skribbl.io - Take turns sketching a secret word while the others race to name it.
  • Draw a Stickman - Your drawing becomes the hero of a short adventure.

Draw and make things

  • AutoDraw - Doodle something and get a cleaner drawing suggested.
  • Quick, Draw! - A machine tries to guess what you are drawing against the clock.
  • Kleki - A free painting program in a browser tab.
  • JS KidPix - The cheerful children's paint program, rebuilt for the web.
  • Streetmix - Design a street lane by lane: bikes, trees, buses, benches.
  • Everyone Draw - A shared, endless canvas of pixels.
  • Wplace - Paint pixel art onto a world map with everyone else.

Gloriously pointless

  • Pointer Pointer - Finds a photo of someone pointing at your cursor.
  • Staggering Beauty - Wiggle the worm. Shake it harder.
  • Zombo.com - A famous welcome that never quite gets anywhere.
  • Eel Slap - Exactly what it sounds like.
  • Cat Bounce - Cats, bouncing.
  • Nyan Cat - The rainbow-trailing cat, forever.
  • Popcat - Click a cat and add to your country's count.
  • Is It Christmas? - Answers one question every day of the year.
  • Is My Computer On? - Answers another.
  • Has the Large Hadron Collider Destroyed the World Yet? - A reassuring status page.
  • Bouncing DVD Logo - Wait for it to hit the corner.
  • Pixels Fighting - Two colours battle for the screen.
  • Smash the Walls - Break walls together with everyone else online.
  • Passive Aggressive Passwords - A password checker with opinions about you.

Made by a machine

  • This Person Does Not Exist - A new invented face on every load.
  • Which Face Is Real? - Pick the real photo out of a pair.
  • This Word Does Not Exist - Invented words with confident definitions.
  • InspiroBot - Motivational posters that go strange quickly.
  • Library of Babel - Every possible page of text, already written, waiting to be searched.

Questions to argue about

  • Moral Machine - Decide what a driverless car should do when every choice harms someone.
  • Philosophy Experiments - Short interactive tests of your moral and logical intuitions.
  • Any Human Ever - Draws one life at random from everyone who has ever lived.

Pretend to work

  • Hacker Typer - Mash the keyboard and look like a film hacker.
  • GeekTyper - More hacker screens, with themes.
  • FakeUpdate - A full-screen operating system update that never ends.
  • LookingBusy - A screen full of fake dashboards and messages.

The old web

  • Windows 93 - A joke operating system full of hidden programs.
  • 98.js - Windows 98 recreated in the browser, with Paint and Minesweeper.
  • Cameron's World - A collage of GeoCities pages from the 1990s.
  • Space Jam (1996) - A film's website, still online as it was in 1996.
  • OldWeb.today - Browse archived pages in old browsers running in emulation.
  • The Old Net - Visit sites as they were in a chosen year.
  • Web Design Museum - How famous websites and apps looked over the decades.
  • Flash Museum - Thousands of Flash games and cartoons, playable again.
  • My90sTV - Channel-surf 1990s television, adverts included.
  • Internet Archive - The web's memory, plus old books, films and software to run.

Rabbit holes to read

  • Random Wikipedia article - Start anywhere.
  • Damn Interesting - Long true stories from science and history.
  • The Public Domain Review - Essays on strange and beautiful works from the past that anyone may now reuse.
  • Open Culture - Free courses, films, audiobooks and lectures.
  • kottke.org - A daily blog of links about art, science and design since 1998.
  • Nestflix - A catalogue of the fictional films and shows that appear inside real ones.

More collections

  • Bored.com - A large, hand-picked directory of fun websites.
  • PointlessSites - Silly sites, facts and pictures, gathered since the early web.
  • Random.Express - Browser games, simulators and toys in one place.
  • ooh.directory - Thousands of good blogs sorted by subject.
  • r/InternetIsBeautiful - A community that shares useful and pleasing websites.
  • Chrome Experiments - The archive of creative experiments made for the browser.
